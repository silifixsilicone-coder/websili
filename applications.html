Sealing Applications & Use Cases

From architectural glass curtain walls to interior joinery and sanitary sealing.

Fenestration Sealing

Windows & Doors Perimeter Sealing

Windows and exterior door assemblies represent the most common vulnerability for wind-driven rainwater ingress, acoustic loss, and draft leaks. WELFIX silicone sealants accommodate the differential thermal movement between masonry/concrete walls and UPVC, timber, or aluminum frames without breaking adhesion.

Key Performance: 100% waterproof barrier, acoustic dampening, dust isolation.
Recommended Sealant: WELFIX Acrylic 300 ml / Weatherproof

Architectural Glazing

Glass & Frames Glazing Solutions

Supported by Welfix's deep glass heritage, WELFIX sealants provide exceptional bonding to non-porous glass surfaces, aluminum extrusions, and storefront displays. Our formulations cure into an elastic rubber gasket that cushions glass against structural shocks, wind pressure, and thermal stress.

Key Performance: High tensile adhesion to float glass, laminated glass, and anodized profiles.
Recommended Sealant: WELFIX Acetic Silicone / Acrylic

Sanitary & Wet Zones

Kitchen & Bathroom Waterproof Sealing

Bathrooms and kitchens require dependable waterproof seals that resist constant moisture, hot water steam, cleaning chemicals, and standing water. WELFIX silicone forms a protective barrier around undermount basins, shower cubicles, bathtubs, and marble countertop joints.

Key Performance: 100% waterproof barrier, high moisture resistance, durable joint integrity.
Recommended Sealant: WELFIX Acrylic General Purpose / Acetic

Interior Joinery & Finishing

Interior Gap Filling & Decorative Moulding

Achieve clean architectural lines across interior living spaces. WELFIX Acrylic General Purpose Sealant fills expansion seams between baseboards, crown mouldings, architraves, door frames, and gypsum plasterboards with smooth toolability.

Key Performance: Smooth tooling, paintable compatibility, shrink resistant.
Recommended Sealant: WELFIX Acrylic General Purpose (300 ml)

Building Envelope & Facades

Exterior Facades & Cladding Expansion Joints

High-rise building envelopes face intense exposure to direct sun UV rays, extreme diurnal temperature swings, and torrential monsoon downpours. WELFIX Weatherproof Silicone Sealant provides resilient expansion joint sealing for ACP panels, stone cladding, and exterior facade interfaces.

Key Performance: UV resistant, high movement capability, non-chalking formula.
Recommended Sealant: WELFIX Weatherproof Silicone Sealant
