Product Overview

WELFIX Acetic Silicone Sealant is a high-performance acetic cure sealant providing quick skinning, rapid vulcanization, and exceptional adhesive strength on glass, glazed ceramic tiles, vitrified surfaces, and aluminum assemblies. Ideal for sanitary fixtures, bathroom enclosures, and architectural glazing.

Key Features

Fast Curing

Accelerated skin-over and curing profile for quicker installations

High Tensile Bond

Superior adhesion to non-porous glass and ceramic materials

Waterproof & Moisture Safe

100% moisture resistance for wet zones and bathroom enclosures

Non-Porous Surface Adhesion

Engineered specifically for glass, aluminum and glazed porcelain

Application Areas

Glass to Glass Joints
Sanitary & Bathroom Glazing
Aluminum Frame Enclosures
Ceramic & Vitrified Tiles
