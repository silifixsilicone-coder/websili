Our Purpose

Stronger Bonds. Better Spaces.

WELFIX Solutions is a dedicated provider of silicone sealants and construction sealing solutions. We focus on bridging the gap between high-end industrial sealing performance and practical contractor accessibility.

Whether sealing high-rise curtain walls, installing premium aluminum and glass windows, or waterproofing sanitary fixtures in luxury residences, our sealants are engineered to withstand thermal movement, UV radiation, and heavy rainfall without losing elasticity or bond integrity.

Authentic Operations & Verification

We believe in honest, transparent specifications. Our products undergo rigorous batch evaluations to ensure that pack sizes, curing characteristics, and flexibility parameters match professional building standards.

Our Approach

How We Ensure Joint Reliability

A successful sealing joint requires the right formulation, reliable elasticity, and weather resistance. Here is the engineering approach behind WELFIX.

01

High Flexibility

Maintains permanent elastomeric flexibility to absorb expansion and contraction movements caused by fluctuating weather.

02

Durable Adhesion

Tenacious mechanical and chemical bonding to architectural glass, anodized aluminum, ceramics, and PVC frames.

03

Waterproof Barrier

Forms an impermeable seal that protects against wind-driven torrential rains, sanitary moisture, and humidity leaks.

04

Weather & UV Resilient

Specially stabilized against ultraviolet degradation, preventing chalking, embrittlement, cracking, and premature aging.
